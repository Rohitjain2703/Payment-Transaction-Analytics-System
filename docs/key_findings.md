# Payment Method Analysis — Key Findings

UPI has the highest transaction share at approximately 47.79%.

Debit Card contributes approximately 21.27% of total transactions.

Credit Card contributes approximately 21.26% of total transactions.

Net Banking has the lowest transaction share at approximately 9.68%.

UPI has the highest total transaction amount among the payment method types.

Credit Card has the highest average transaction amount at approximately 23,550.21.

Payment method data contains duplicate payment method IDs.

Important payment method fields contain missing values.

Transaction data contains missing payment method IDs, affecting payment method mapping.

Transaction status values contain inconsistent formats such as different cases and extra spaces.
